## Sacred Reciprocity

### What It Is

Sacred reciprocity is the understanding that **giving and receiving are one flow**, not two separate transactions.

It's the difference between:
- **Transaction**: "I give you X, you give me Y" (trade, debt, scarcity)
- **Reciprocity**: "I give to the flow, the flow gives to me" (trust, abundance, relationship)

### How It Works

```
Individual Need ──→ Community Gives ──→ Need Met
        ↑                                  │
        │                                  ↓
    Gratitude ←────── Individual Receives ←┘
        │
        ↓
Individual Has Surplus ──→ Community Receives
        │
        ↓
    Circle Continues...
```

**Key Insight**: You don't have to give back to the same person you received from. You give to the flow. The flow gives back to you—often in unexpected ways.

### In Practice

On Infin.Love, this looks like:

- **Using the site** (receiving)
- **Contributing code** (giving)
- **Sharing with others** (giving)
- **Financial support** (giving - voluntary)
- **Bug reports** (giving feedback)
- **Community support** (giving time)

All are valid. All are honored. There is no "should."

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Accessibility First, Always

**Every feature must be accessible or it doesn't ship.**

We will never compromise accessibility for:
- Speed of development
- Visual appeal
- Technical complexity
- Resource constraints

If we can't make it accessible, we don't build it.

### 2. Privacy as Default

**We collect the minimum, protect the maximum.**

Principles:
- No tracking without explicit consent
- No data sold, ever
- No surveillance features
- Transparency about what we collect
- User control over their data

**Why**: Privacy is dignity. Surveillance is violence.

### 3. Performance as Care

**Fast sites show respect for users.**

We optimize because:
- Time is precious
- Bandwidth costs money
- Older devices deserve access
- Slow sites exclude people
- Performance is accessibility

Target: < 1s load time on 3G.

### 4. Open Source as Practice

**Our code is a gift to the commons.**

We open source because:
- Knowledge multiplies when shared
- Community improves everything
- Transparency builds trust
- Future generations benefit
- It's the right thing to do

License: Sacred Commons (permissive with values)

### 5. Community Over Code

**People matter more than features.**

When choosing between:
- New feature vs. better documentation → Documentation
- Cool tech vs. maintainability → Maintainability
- Speed vs. community input → Community
- Growth vs. values → Values

**We choose people.**

### 6. Simplicity as Sophistication

**The simplest solution that works is the best solution.**

Avoid:
- Unnecessary frameworks
- Over-engineering
- Premature optimization
- Feature creep
- Complexity for complexity's sake

Embrace:
- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS
- Progressive enhancement
- Clear, readable code
- Minimal dependencies

### 7. Sustainability Over Growth

**Slow and steady wins the race.**

We prefer:
- Sustainable pace over burnout
- Quality over quantity
- Depth over breadth
- Long-term health over short-term metrics
- Community care over rapid scaling

**Marathon, not sprint.**

## Sacred Economics in Practice

### How We Handle Money

**Voluntary Reciprocity Model:**

1. **Services are free** - No paywalls, no premium tiers
2. **Support is voluntary** - Give what feels right
3. **Transparency** - Where money goes is public
4. **Redistribution** - Surplus supports other projects
5. **No extractive monetization** - No ads, no data selling

**Financial Flow:**
```
Supporters → Ko-fi/GitHub Sponsors
    ↓
Infin.Love receives
    ↓
Covers: Hosting, domain, tools
    ↓
Surplus → Contributors, other gift projects
```

### What We Don't Do

❌ **Ads** - Attention is sacred, not for sale
❌ **Premium features** - Access is universal
❌ **Data sales** - Privacy is non-negotiable
❌ **Artificial scarcity** - No limiting access
❌ **Guilt trips** - Give freely or don't

### What We Do

✅ **Request support** - Clearly, without shame
✅ **Show gratitude** - Thank every contributor
✅ **Spend wisely** - Respect what's given
✅ **Share transparently** - Public financial info
✅ **Give forward** - Support others when able
