# Project Roadmap 🗺️

> *The path ahead, guided by love and community wisdom.*

---

## Roadmap Philosophy

This roadmap is a **living document**—not a rigid plan. It reflects our current vision while remaining open to:
- Community input and priorities
- Emerging needs and insights
- Sacred timing (not rushing)
- Resources and capacity
- Alignment with values

**Principles:**
- **Quality over speed**: Features done right, not rushed
- **Community-driven**: You shape this roadmap
- **Values-aligned**: Every feature serves sacred reciprocity
- **Sustainable**: Never at the expense of maintainer wellbeing
- **Flexible**: Adapt as we learn and grow

## Roadmap Overview

### Timeline Legend

- **✅ Complete**: Shipped and live
- **🚧 In Progress**: Actively being worked on
- **📋 Planned**: Committed to, timeline set
- **💭 Under Consideration**: Exploring, not committed
- **🌟 Community Requested**: Suggested by community
- **⏳ Waiting On**: Blocked or depends on something else

### Priority Levels

- **P0 (Critical)**: Core functionality, security, accessibility
- **P1 (High)**: Major features, significant improvements
- **P2 (Medium)**: Nice-to-haves, enhancements
- **P3 (Low)**: Future possibilities, experimental

### Potential Major Features

#### Gift Circle Management Platform (💭 Future)
**Scope:**
- Create and manage local circles
- Share resources and skills
- Track gifts given/received (non-transactional)
- Organize gatherings and events
- Private but interconnected

**Considerations:**
- Privacy-first design
- No monetization of connections
- Accessible to all
- Sustainable to maintain

#### Decentralized Architecture (💭 Future)
**Scope:**
- Explore blockchain/Web3 for gift tracking
- IndieWeb principles (own your data)
- ActivityPub integration (fediverse)
- P2P connections (no central server)

**Considerations:**
- Complexity vs. benefit
- Environmental impact (energy use)
- Accessibility concerns
- Alignment with values

#### Integration Ecosystem (💭 Future)
**Scope:**
- Calendar integrations (Google, Apple)
- Social media sharing
- Payment platform integrations (ethical only)
- CRM for facilitators
- Analytics and insights

**Considerations:**
- Privacy implications
- Dependency on third parties
- Maintenance burden
- Value to community
