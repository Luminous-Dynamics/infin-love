# Sacred Covenant of Conduct 💜

## Our Intention

Infin.Love is a sacred space dedicated to practicing infinite love through reciprocity, generosity, and trust. This Code of Conduct is not a set of rules to enforce, but a sacred covenant we hold together—a commitment to creating conditions where all beings can flourish.

## Our Sacred Principles

### 1. **Love as Foundation**
Every interaction springs from love. We approach each other, the code, and ourselves with compassion, patience, and kindness.

### 2. **Sacred Reciprocity**
We give freely and receive graciously. Contributions are gifts, not transactions. Feedback is offered with care, received with openness.

### 3. **Radical Inclusion**
This space welcomes all beings regardless of:
- Age, body size, visible or invisible disability
- Ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression
- Level of experience, education, socio-economic status
- Nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Technical skill or contribution size

### 4. **Dignity & Respect**
Every person carries inherent worth. We honor each other's dignity, listen deeply, and speak with care.

### 5. **Trust Over Control**
We choose trust as our operating system. We assume good intentions, ask clarifying questions, and extend grace.

## Practices We Cultivate

### **In Communication:**
- Speak from personal experience using "I" statements
- Listen with full presence and open curiosity
- Ask questions before making assumptions
- Acknowledge impact even when intent was good
- Practice patience with language barriers and learning curves

### **In Collaboration:**
- Celebrate diverse perspectives and approaches
- Welcome constructive feedback as a gift
- Disagree gracefully without being disagreeable
- Credit others' ideas and contributions
- Support newcomers with patience and encouragement

### **In Conflict:**
- Address issues directly with compassion
- Focus on behavior and impact, not character
- Seek to understand before seeking to be understood
- Engage in dialogue, not debate
- Invite mediation when direct resolution isn't working

### **In Community:**
- Share knowledge freely
- Lift others up without putting anyone down
- Create space for all voices, especially marginalized ones
- Honor boundaries and consent
- Practice gratitude and appreciation

## Behaviors That Don't Belong Here

We cannot practice sacred reciprocity while engaging in:

### **Harmful Communication:**
- Violent, threatening, or intimidating language
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments
- Harassment, stalking, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, inflammatory comments, or deliberate disruption
- Publishing others' private information without consent

### **Discriminatory Behavior:**
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention
- Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or ableist comments
- Jokes or remarks that demean or exclude
- Stereotyping or making assumptions about groups
- Denying someone's lived experience

### **Exploitation:**
- Manipulation or taking advantage of others
- Pressure or coercion in any form
- Appropriation of others' work without credit
- Commercial exploitation of community members
- Bad faith participation or concern trolling

## When Harm Occurs

We're all learning. Mistakes happen. Growth is sacred.

### **If You Cause Harm:**
1. Listen with openness when someone shares their experience
2. Acknowledge the impact without being defensive
3. Apologize sincerely
4. Ask what repair looks like
5. Make genuine amends and change behavior

### **If You Experience Harm:**
1. You have the right to disengage and protect yourself
2. Consider addressing it directly if you feel safe to do so
3. Reach out to project maintainers if you need support
4. We will take your report seriously and act with care

### **Reporting**

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this covenant:

**Email**: tristan.stoltz@gmail.com

**What to include**:
- Your contact information
- Description of what happened (who, what, when, where)
- Any additional context that would be helpful
- Whether you want the report to remain confidential

**What happens next**:
1. We'll acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
2. We'll review the situation with care and confidentiality
3. We'll take appropriate action, which may include:
   - Facilitated dialogue
   - Temporary or permanent ban from the community
   - Reporting to GitHub or relevant authorities
4. We'll follow up with you about the outcome

## Enforcement Philosophy

We practice **restorative justice** rather than punitive punishment:

- **First**: Assume good intent and seek mutual understanding
- **Then**: Invite reflection and genuine repair
- **If needed**: Create boundaries to protect the community
- **Always**: Center the wellbeing of those harmed

Enforcement will be:
- **Proportional**: Response matches the severity of harm
- **Consistent**: Similar situations handled similarly
- **Transparent**: Community understands our processes
- **Accountable**: Maintainers are also held to this covenant

## Scope

This covenant applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub repo, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- All community spaces (if we create additional forums)
- Public spaces when representing the project
- Private communication when it affects community dynamics

## Our Commitment

The project maintainers commit to:
- Upholding these principles in our own behavior
- Addressing violations fairly and compassionately
- Protecting the safety and dignity of all community members
- Continuously learning and improving our practices
- Being accountable for our decisions and actions

## Evolution

This covenant is alive and will evolve. We welcome feedback on how to better serve the community and practice sacred reciprocity.

## Attribution & Inspiration

- The Contributor Covenant

- Principles of Nonviolent Communication
- Sacred reciprocity and gift economy traditions
- Restorative justice practices

It is offered in the spirit of co-creation and shared responsibility.
