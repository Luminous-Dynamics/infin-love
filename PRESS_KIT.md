Infin.Love transforms how communities exchange value. Moving beyond transactional thinking, we've created a beautiful, accessible platform where people form "gift circles"—communities built on freely giving and receiving rather than keeping score. In a world dominated by transactional thinking, Infin.Love offers a revolutionary alternative: sacred reciprocity. Comprehensive documentation (9,000+ lines) makes contributing welcoming for all skill levels.

This isn't just a platform—it's a living experiment in post-capitalist economics, proving that communities can thrive when we lead with generosity instead of scarcity.

## ❓ Media FAQ

### What is Infin.Love?

Infin.Love is an open-source web platform that enables communities to practice "sacred reciprocity"—freely giving and receiving without keeping score. Think of it as social infrastructure for trust-based economies.

### What are "gift circles"?

Gift circles are communities where members share time, skills, resources, or wisdom without expectation of direct return. Unlike transactions (I give you X, you give me Y), gifts flow freely through the circle. You give when you can, receive when you need.

### How does this differ from barter or time banks?

Barter and time banks still operate on exchange logic—tracking who gives what to whom. Gift circles operate on flow and trust. There's no ledger, no scorekeeping. This shifts the relational dynamic from obligation to generosity.

### Is this financially sustainable?

The project itself operates on gift principles—open source, volunteer-driven, accepting donations via Ko-fi. Interestingly, by removing the pressure to monetize, we've built something far more valuable: a healthy community and sustainable pace.

### How technical do I need to be to use it?

Zero technical skill required to participate in gift circles. The website is designed for everyone—WCAG 2.1 AA accessible, mobile-friendly, works on any device.

To contribute to the open-source project, we welcome all skill levels. Our QUICK_START.md gets you contributing in 5 minutes, and ONBOARDING.md provides comprehensive guidance.

### How do you prevent abuse?

Gift circles work best at community scale where relationships and reputation matter. We also provide governance tools and Code of Conduct based on restorative justice principles. Trust is earned over time through consistent generosity.

### What's your business model?

We don't have one in the traditional sense. The project is open source (MIT license), accepting voluntary gifts via Ko-fi. We believe the value created through healthier communities and economic alternatives far exceeds what we could extract through monetization.

### Can I use this for my local community?

Absolutely! The platform is designed to support both online and local circles. See [COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md) for guidance on starting local gift circles.

### How can I cover this story?

We'd be honored! Feel free to use any materials in this press kit. For interviews, additional info, or high-res assets, contact tristan.stoltz@gmail.com.

### What's next for Infin.Love?

Our [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) outlines 2025 plans including: multi-language support, enhanced circle management tools, mobile app, and deeper gift circle facilitation features. All decisions made transparently with community input.
